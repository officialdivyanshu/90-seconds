      01
      
Four steps.
Two minutes total.

    

    
The order matters — the thinking time is deliberately short so you learn to structure a thought while you're already talking, not before.

    

      

        0:00
        1
        
Take the topic

        
One line, picked at random. Films, hot takes, lifestyle, explain-it-simply. You don't get to choose, and you don't get to skip.

      

      

        0:00 → 0:30
        2
        
Think fast

        
Thirty seconds to read it properly and find an angle. Enough to pick a direction, not enough to write a script.

      

      

        0:30 → 2:00
        3
        
Camera opens

        
It opens on its own. Ninety seconds, one continuous take, no pause button. Talk until it stops.

      

      

        AFTER 2:00
        4
        
Read the report

        
Speech becomes text, text becomes numbers, numbers become one specific thing to fix in your next round.

      

    

  






  

    

      02
      
What comes back

    

    
One card, one round. Counting happens in code so the numbers are exact — the model reads them and tells you what they mean.

What you won't find here

          
No scores, no streaks, no dashboard. Every round is judged on its own — the point is the ninety seconds, not the graph afterwards.

        

      

    

  






  

    

      03
      
Under the hood

    

    
Built to stay fast when a hundred people press record at the same time.

    

      

        Topics
        
Written ahead of time

        
Thousands of prompts generated offline and stored. Serving one is a single indexed lookup, so the clock never waits on a model.

      

      

        Transcript
        
Whisper, self-hosted

        
Runs on your own machine with word-level timestamps. No per-minute billing, no rate limit in the middle of a load test.

      

      

        Feedback
        
Queued, never blocking

        
Recording goes to a job queue and the page gets an ID straight back. The model can take four seconds; the API answers in forty milliseconds.
